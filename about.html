About

I build systems that make businesses work better — connecting strategy, data, and execution across teams.

Hi, I'm Dylan.

I'm currently a Global Partner Manager at Amazon, driving advertiser growth through partner-led strategies and operational improvements across Southeast Asia. I've delivered 5 consecutive quarters of KPI overperformance and co-built 4 Data & AI solutions scaled across countries.

Before Amazon, I spent nearly 3 years at The Lab — a company of creatives — where I progressed from Account Executive to Strategic Partnership Manager. I managed clients like Apple, MUJI, Vero, Sunlife, and Bitis, drove 40% of annual company revenue, and launched a 1,000sqm flagship bakery coordinating 10+ teams.

I started my career at PMAX Vietnam in digital advertising, co-managing campaigns for FE CREDIT, VPBank & VAS Education, and winning Gold & Bronze at SMARTIES Vietnam 2021.

What drives me: taking complex, messy problems and turning them into clear systems that teams can actually use. Whether that's an AI agent that replaces hours of manual analysis, a partnership strategy for an international brand, or an operational system that scales across markets.

I don't just deliver — I make the delivery process better for next time.
